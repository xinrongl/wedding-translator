- The app is used for the wedding speech for real-time transalation from Chinese to English.
- The app need to listen the real-time voice and segment the voice with absolutely right time and translate into English (in text).
- The app will have a front end app display the translated English in real-time.
- The app can use typescript / python, depending on the project need but prioritize on the perfromance
- The app will use GCP service. The translation can either using GCP translation service or using Gemini Service.
